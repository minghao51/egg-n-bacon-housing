What follows from that table:

- **Hawker presence is the only amenity whose historical state can be measured at all**, and only as completion dates joined by name to each transaction's _nearest_ centre. The shipped distance features never read those dates.
- **Parks, supermarkets, preschools, childcare and malls have no presence dates anywhere in the checkout** (parks and childcare carry only dataset update stamps — an update stamp dates the dataset refresh, not the amenity's presence). ### 3b. Hawker completion mismatch (`snapshotJoinMismatch`)

Each study-window transaction's nearest hawker centre is classified against the completion dates the raw GeoJSON carries (exact rule recorded in the ledger; a year-only value in the transaction's own year is conservatively **not** established as completed):

| Bucket                                                                  | Transactions |      Share |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -----------: | ---------: |
| Nearest centre completed before the transaction                         |      203,172 |     72.29% |
| Nearest centre **not established** as completed at the transaction date |   **77,173** | **27.46%** |
| Nearest centre completion unknown (Tiong Bahru Market, null date)       |          675 |      0.24% |

By legacy period (`snapshotJoinMismatch.byWindow`): the not-established share is **40.4%** of 2015-2019 transactions (n = 101,409), **32.36%** of 2020-2022 (n = 79,140), and **10.55%** of 2023-2026 (n = 100,471). **17,179 transactions (6.11%) have a nearest centre that is `Under Construction`** in the snapshot — one of six named in the ledger (`Bukit Timah Market`, `Chong Pang Hawker Centre & Market`, `Tampines North Hawker Centre`, `Tanglin Halt Hawker Centre & Market`, `Telok Blangah Hawker Centre & Market`, `Yew Tee Hawker Centre`, with completion years 2027, 2029 and 2030 in `futureYearCompletionYears`). For these transactions the shipped distance is measured to a centre that is not operating in the snapshot, and the same value is joined to transactions of every study month; when construction at each site began is not in this checkout, so the distance's meaning at earlier dates is not established.

Limitation of this measure, stated: it classifies only the **nearest** centre by name; centres farther than the nearest are not classified, and completion dates describe building completion, not operating continuity.

## 4. The refit: one recorded specification, units and controls stated

**Estimator** (`associations.estimator`): OLS (statsmodels, HC1 heteroskedasticity-robust covariance, `use_t=False`). Response: **log(resale price), price in nominal Singapore dollars**. Functional form: log-linear; coefficients are log points, and `pctEffectExpMinus1 = 100·(exp(β)−1)` is the approximate percentage difference implied. Standard errors: HC1 robust, unclustered (town and year enter as fixed effects, not clusters). This is a **recorded** specification, not a pre-registered one: the ledger records one specification, only that one was fitted, and all its coefficients are reported — but no dated prior protocol exists in this checkout, so the possibility of specification search cannot be ruled out. The single-specification discipline is disclosed as a limitation, not claimed as a credential.

**Regressors with units:** five binary amenity indicators, each `1` when the shipped distance column is ≤ 1,000 m and `0` otherwise — **all five fitted jointly in the one recorded specification** (no selection, no stepwise search). Controls: floor area (**sqm**, continuous), lease commencement year (**calendar year**, continuous — with the transaction-year fixed effects this is affine-equivalent to remaining lease for 99-year flats, per `associations.estimator.remainingLeaseEquivalence`), storey midpoint (**storey number**, continuous), flat-type fixed effects (7 levels, baseline 1-ROOM), town fixed effects (baseline ANG MO KIO), transaction-year fixed effects (baseline 2015; they absorb nominal drift and inflation). Continuous controls are scaled internally for numerical stability and reported back-transformed to natural units. **Not controlled** (listed in the ledger): flat model, unit condition/renovation/view, block attributes, buyer grants and quota constraints, walking-path distance, amenity quality or operating status, town×year shocks, and the amenity snapshot's own vintage.

**Sample** (`associations.sample`): HDB resale transactions, 2015-01 → 2026-10, **n = 281,020**, k = 51, R² = 0.9157 (adjusted 0.9157), df_resid = 280,969. Exclusions are **reason sets over every HDB row** (990,655): **697,246 rows fail `before-study-window` alone**, 790 fail `terminal-partial-month-after-cutoff` alone, and **11,599 rows carry a six-reason set** (before-study-window + all five `missing-source:<amenity>-distance`, because their locations have no coordinates and every amenity column is null); the total `before-study-window` count is 708,845 because the six-reason rows are also pre-window (`excludedRowsByReasonCount`: 698,036 single-reason rows, 11,599 six-reason rows). **Rows excluded inside the window: 0** (`excludedInWindowRows` is empty). Condo rows are excluded by scope, not silently: 245,655 rows, 0 before 2021-01, 245,234 in the window, 421 in the terminal month (`condoScope`) — **no condo model is fitted** and no cross-segment claim is made.

### 4b. What the associations are and are not

Every coefficient above is a **conditional association in one cross-section**: transactions whose location is nearer a snapshot amenity differ in price from transactions farther away, conditional on flat type, town, year, floor area, lease commencement and storey. Town and transaction-year fixed effects enter **separately**, so the amenity coefficients are identified from within-town differences in indicator status after removing year-to-year price movements common to all towns; they do **not** condition on joint town-year cells, and town-specific time shocks, unit quality and the snapshot's own vintage remain uncontrolled (`associations.estimator.notControlled`). The legacy figures these numbers most resemble — XGBoost/SHAP feature-importance shares — are **different quantities from a different (retired) model**, and this pack does not present its associations as confirming, refuting or replacing them.

## 6. What this pack does not establish

- **No causal premium.** No coefficient measures what a hawker centre, park, mall, supermarket or station _does to_ a price. Nothing here identifies why two otherwise-similar flats differ, so nothing here is a "premium of X%".
- **No temporal claim of any kind.** One snapshot is joined to 2015–2026 transactions alike; no per-period model is fitted, and the hawker completion-date audit is a mismatch measure, not a vintage-corrected feature.
- **No importance ranking.** Feature importance belonged to the retired XGBoost/SHAP model; conditional associations cannot rank "importance" and are not offered as doing so.
- **No interaction or synergy estimate.** The amenity-count coefficient is not an interaction that "exceeds the sum of individual effects".
- **No condo statement.** The private-residential source starts 2021-01; the legacy condo claims span 2015–2026.
- **No completeness claim.** The raw MP25 mall layer is absent from this checkout (fetched live at ingestion); hawker completion dates describe completion, not operating continuity; sources outside this checkout are not assessed.
- **No pre-registration credential.** The ledger records one specification and only that one was fitted, with all coefficients reported; there is no dated prior protocol in this checkout, so specification search cannot be ruled out. The single-specification discipline is a disclosed limitation (§4), not evidence that the specification was chosen before any result was seen.
