## Gate and clause-by-clause disposition

> Retain January 2024-January 2025 as its own historical window. Rerun the stated hotspot method, disclose persistence/instability and sample changes. Keep rental hotspot results separate from the transaction-activity product.

- **“Retain January 2024-January 2025 as its own historical window.”** The pinned `gateWindow` is 2024-01 through 2025-01 (`gateWindow.first`, `gateWindow.last`, `gateWindow.monthCount`); it is retained separately from the live cutoff. The file population in the window is `gateWindow.rowsInFile`; joined and unmatched counts are `joinMeasure.window.matchedRows` and `joinMeasure.window.unmatchedRows`. The unmatched rows are retained as a reported population, not silently removed.
- **“Rerun the stated hotspot method, disclose persistence/instability and sample changes.”** The joined window rows were measured using H3 resolution 8, median `monthly_rent` per cell, Getis-Ord Gi\* with 99 seeded permutations, KNN k=8 and row-standardised weights (`hotspotStatistic.method`). The legacy does not specify which esda z output its thresholds refer to; both are reported, not adjudicated as the legacy reading. The two bases' counts and ranges are under `hotspotStatistic.analyticZ`, `hotspotStatistic.simulationZ`, and `hotspotStatistic.cellCounts`. Persistence is not measurable from this one window; the legacy transition probabilities have no linked artefact and no transition implementation was recovered (`notMeasurable.persistence`). Sample change is explicitly measured in `joinMeasure.window`, including unmatched records, and in `joinMeasure.unmatchedPopulation`.
- **“Keep rental hotspot results separate from the transaction-activity product.”** The rental-only output is `app/public/data/analytics/rental_hotspots_2024-01_2025-01.json.gz` (`rentalAsset.path`), a new asset. The producer reads the transaction-activity product read-only and writes no rental results there (`shippedAssetSeparation.spatialAnalysis`). The destination `spatial_analysis.json.gz` remains RS11's transaction-activity product.
