## Gate (verbatim)

> Refresh last. Every quantitative sentence links to a reviewed edition and exact population/window/unit. Omit or explicitly flag blocked dependencies; never summarize unresolved models as settled conclusions.

### Clause-by-clause disposition

1. **Refresh last.** Fourteen of the fifteen series carry a signed edition; the fifteenth is this page, which the register records as `self: true` with no signing state of its own. The register is measured at 2026-11-20 against the registry's own list, not against a remembered count: `seriesRegister.summary.seriesCount` (15), `.signedCount` (14), `.unsignedSeries` (`[]`), `.selfSeries` (`["findings"]`).
2. **Every quantitative sentence links to a reviewed edition and exact population/window/unit.** The edition text's quantitative sentences are drawn from this ledger's register fields, and each one names the population it counts, the window it is measured at, and the unit it counts. Per-series populations, windows and units are _quoted verbatim_ from the series' own ledger (`seriesRegister.rows.<series>.descriptors`), and the producer fails the build if a quote is not a literal substring of the field it cites.
3. **Omit or explicitly flag blocked dependencies.** `dependencies.blocked` is `[]`, and it is the _result_ of a stated rule rather than an assertion: a dependency is blocked when its edition is not signed, or its measured text sha256 differs from its pin, or its ledger's canonical content sha256 differs from the attestation pin. `dependencies.blockedCheck` records the row counts the rule was applied to (`rowsEvaluated` 15, `siblingRowsEvaluated` 14) and the exclusions — one ledger whose bytes (not whose content) moved after signature, and the page's own row, which is excluded by identity rather than by status (`selfRule`).
4. **Never summarize unresolved models as settled conclusions.** No sibling measurement appears in this page as a finding. Each frozen findings sentence is disposed by naming the edition that owns it and re-reading that edition's own ledger entry for the same figure (`crossLedgerCheck`), and the two sentences that need a joint model are recorded as not measurable instead of being reworded into a conclusion.

### The pin test, and the one row that fails it at the byte level

The text-hash test is the one that caught a stale signature earlier in the programme, so it is a field, not a sentence: `seriesRegister.rows.<series>.editionFile.matchesPin`. Fourteen of fourteen frozen texts hash to the sha256 their entry records.

The ledger test is the same test against the sha256 all three attestations share (`artifactSha256`), and it is measured twice per ledger:

- **Raw bytes** — `seriesRegister.rows.<series>.ledger.rawMatchesPin`. Thirteen of fourteen match.
- **Parsed content** — `seriesRegister.rows.<series>.ledger.contentMatchesPin`, the sha256 of the canonical re-serialisation. Fourteen of fourteen match.

`data-sources` is the row where those two disagree, and the ledger records the disagreement rather than smoothing it: its on-disk ledger is `21d74a7f233d972792b7027b3fba8573852215b54e463b072d137dc8a729a81f` while the attestation pins `65ce7a10c257d13fd4250b2e8fd1519f8c35718497379aa103effc5594bfe8ae`, and canonical re-serialisation of the parsed file reproduces the pin exactly (`seriesRegister.rows.data-sources.ledger.serialisationDrift`). The cause is a post-signature formatter run that collapsed arrays onto single lines without changing the parsed JSON; the same commit added the `docs/implementation/artifacts/` prettier exclusion that keeps the tree out of the formatter's reach afterwards. The producer fails on any mismatch that is _not_ declared in `KNOWN_SERIALISATION_DRIFT`, fails if the declared row's raw hash moves, and fails if the content hash ever stops reproducing the pin — so this is a pinned disclosure, not a waivable tolerance. The row stays in the register with `rawMatchesPin: false` visible; it is not "fixed" by regenerating someone else's signed artefact.

## What this pack does not establish

- **It establishes nothing about any study's subject.** No coefficient, count or band from a sibling edition appears here as a finding; the numbers in this pack are register facts (how many series, how many signed, how many attestations, how many claim items) plus the hashes of the bytes on disk.
- **It does not verify a sibling edition's reasoning.** A row saying `textMatchesPin: true` says the frozen text is the text the attestations cover. It says nothing about whether that text is right, and nothing about the review it received beyond the record's own existence.
- **It does not adjudicate the `data-sources` byte drift.** It measures the two hashes, reproduces the pin from canonical serialisation, and names the cause; whether the signature continues to cover a reformatted file is a decision for the record's owner, not for a summary page.
- **It does not make the persona promises measurable.** The persona file carries no quantitative token to bind; its recommended-reading slugs are resolved against the register and left at that.
- **It does not complete the programme.** The register's own wording allows a narrower honest summary, and this is one: fourteen signed editions and one page that names what it cannot say.
- **The absence scan is bounded.** Its roots, suffixes, file count and exclusions are fields; a file outside them is outside the measurement, not established absent.
