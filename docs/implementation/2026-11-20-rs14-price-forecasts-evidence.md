2. **Use rolling-origin validation, leakage checks, naive trend/seasonal baselines, error metrics and empirical prediction-interval coverage.** Rolling-origin validation covers the naive baselines at the 23 origins recorded in `rollingOrigin`; the stored model has one forecast origin in this asset, so its rolling-origin performance is not measurable from the asset. The ledger records its leakage screens with their limits: they are screens, not findings about origin. The primary scoring compares stored forecasts with zero-change (last-value), six-month trend-persistence, and seasonal-naive baselines. The accuracy table reports MAE, MAPE on implied price levels, and bias; further metrics and full definitions are in `forecastScoring.primary.methods`. Stored intervals empirically cover 1 of 35 comparable areas (2.8571%); 0.95 is an assumed nominal level because the asset does not specify one. This is cross-sectional coverage of one stored forecast period, not repeated time-series coverage.
3. **Name R-squared correctly.** `model_r2` is an R-squared fit statistic (reported by the asset), not forecast accuracy. The article’s segment table calls R-squared “Accuracy”; its five per-segment rows are withdrawn, not re-measured, because no corresponding segment outputs or split provenance are present in the bounded evidence. No structural screen establishes how a series was produced; where screened, the origin is unverified.
4. **Score expired 2025 forecasts where comparable outcomes exist.** The expired 2025-07-01 projections are scored against observed planning-area median prices for 2025-06 to 2025-12 under the explicitly stated primary anchor convention. Comparable scoring population, exclusions, alternative anchor readings, basis sensitivities, and per-area details are in `forecastScoring`. No stored date is advanced.
5. **Never advance a stored forecast date or reuse BUY/SELL prompts as current advice.** The stored forecast date remains 2025-07-01 throughout this record. The asset’s BUY labels are withdrawn as current advice and are not reproduced as a recommendation. The shipped app describes the signal as archive/not advice and withholds BUY/SELL prompts, as recorded in `appConsumption`.
