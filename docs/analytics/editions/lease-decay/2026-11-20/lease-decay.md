## What the numbers are—and are not

This edition is a measured inventory of the available frame and an audit of inherited statements, not an estimate of a causal lease effect. Price response distinctions matter: `price` is total SGD price; `psf` is measured SGD per square foot; `derivedPsm` is computed as `price / floor_area_sqm` and expressed per square metre. The stored `Unit Price ($ PSM)` column is present and populated on the private rows: 122,017 non-null values frame-wide and 0 on HDB rows, stored as a string with thousands separators, of which a plain numeric read parses 0 (`responseDecision.columnsExamined["Unit Price ($ PSM)"].frameNonNull`, `.hdbNonNull`, `.storage`, `.numericParsedNonNull`). That is not an absent column, an absent value, or a measured zero transaction (`responseDecision.fourStatesNote`).

The underlying platinum dataset is described from parquet metadata, not by an inferred column roster: the file has 1,236,310 rows and 97 columns (`inputs.data/pipeline/04_platinum/unified_dataset.parquet`). Its HDB subset contains 990,655 rows (`cutoff.hdbRowsTotal`). The figures in band and town tables apply only to their stated window and definitions. They do not make every row in the source file part of every statistic. The measured band window is 2017-01 through the cutoff 2026-10 (`bandTable.window.start`, `.end`; `cutoff.lastCompleteMonth`). The town-window dates are the same, but that is a newly declared measurement window, not a window recovered from the frozen text (`townTable.definition.window`, `.windowStatus`).

Four states remain distinct: null, recorded zero, absent row, and absent column. A sentinel string is a fifth distinct state: a present value that encodes missingness; `remaining_lease` uses the string `'nan'` this way (`responseDecision.fourStatesNote`, `leaseAgeValidation.storedColumn.formatCounts`). On HDB rows, `remaining_lease` has 0 Arrow nulls and 708,845 sentinel strings; it has no numeric zero values. The other stored formats are counted separately: 37,153 bare integer strings, 20,054 `N years` strings, and 224,603 `N years M month(s)` strings; unparsed values: 0 (`leaseAgeValidation.storedColumn.formatCounts`, `.nullRows`, `.rowsEqualToZero`). The absent-column state is counted: 3 columns the frozen text names — `remaining_lease_months`, `resale_price`, and `price_per_sqm` — are absent from the shipped 97-column schema (`responseDecision.absentColumns.count`, `.columns`, `.shippedSchemaColumns`). The interactive asset has seven age-bin rows absent from its expected set (`interactiveAsset.ageCoverage`). The legacy frames at `data/parquets/L1/housing_hdb_transaction.parquet` and `data/parquets/L3/housing_unified.parquet` are absent paths in this checkout; this does not imply those source datasets never existed (`absenceScans.l1Frame`, `.l3Frame`).

All windows are cut at the measured last-complete month, not at an assumed calendar endpoint. The terminal month 2026-11 has 790 transactions, equal to 0.3718 of the trailing-twelve-month median of 2,125.0; the preceding month 2026-10 has 2,173 transactions, equal to 1.0226 of that median. ### Lease-age validation

The derivation uses transaction year and month against `lease_commence_date`, at month precision. The formula is recorded directly in the ledger: age is the transaction year-month less commencement year, and derived remaining years are the 99-year assumption less that age (`leaseAgeValidation.derivation.formula`, `.precision`, `.tenureAssumedYears`). The tenure assumption is checked against the stored column where that column exists rather than being treated as an unquestioned source fact.

The stored `remaining_lease` is a string column. Its documented parser accepts the exact `'nan'` sentinel, bare integer years, `N years`, and `N years M month(s)` (months converted as `N + M/12`); it leaves Arrow nulls distinct and fails if a non-null value matches none of these formats (`leaseAgeValidation.storedColumn.parseRule`, `.formatCounts`). Counts are: 708,845 sentinel strings, 37,153 bare integer strings, 20,054 years-only strings, 224,603 years-and-months strings, and 0 unparsed; all 990,655 HDB rows are Arrow-non-null (`leaseAgeValidation.storedColumn.formatCounts`, `.nonNullRows`, `.nullRows`). The parser yields 281,810 real values. Comparing those with `99 − (transaction_year − lease_commence_date)`, 281,674 are within one year (99.9517%), with maximum absolute deviation 14.9167 years. The band counts are 281,674 within one year, 135 over one through two years, 0 over two through five years, and 1 over five years (`leaseAgeValidation.agreement.rowsCompared`, `.shareWithin1Year`, `.maxAbsDeviation`, `.deviationBandCounts`).

There are 51 rows with negative derived lease age under the stated tenure assumption; they are counted rather than excluded (`leaseAgeValidation.exceptions.rowsWithNegativeLeaseAge`). The agreement comparison uses transaction year as specified, while other derived age calculations retain month precision (`leaseAgeValidation.agreement.comparison`, `leaseAgeValidation.derivation.precision`). In the analytic window, the observed derived range is 38.25 to 97.4167 years (`sampleClaim.measuredLeaseRangeYearsWindow`).
