## What the figures mean

This edition keeps separate a reported legacy quantity, a measured row or value in the current data, and an inference. A legacy number can be quoted faithfully without being adopted as a result. A count describes the population and rule under which it was measured, not an implicit population outside those boundaries. No coefficient below is translated into a willingness-to-pay estimate, school-choice recommendation, or admissions effect.

The underlying parquet read was metadata-only when checking schemas. The unified dataset contains 1,236,310 rows and 97 columns (`inputs.data/pipeline/04_platinum/unified_dataset.parquet`). The bronze school directory has 337 rows and 32 columns (`inputs.data/pipeline/01_bronze/raw_school_directory.parquet`). The analysis does not recalculate any school coordinates or rerun the pipeline. Its measured association uses existing distance columns in the unified dataset; estimates and sample properties are taken from the reproducible analysis recorded in the evidence ledger (`association`, `columnHealth`).

Missingness categories are not interchangeable. A null is a present field with no value; a recorded zero is an exact stored zero; an absent row is a row the measured frame contains that the measured study screen does not; an absent column is a name missing from the inspected schemas. Each state is counted separately (`columnHealth.stateCounts`): 3 absent column names, 81,200 null cells across the seven school fields (11,600 per column, through the cutoff bound), 969,443 recorded-zero cells through the bound, and 1,078,868 rows outside the measured screen (operands recorded at `columnHealth.stateCounts.absentRow.operands`: 1,235,099 frame rows through the bound minus 156,231 measured screen rows; the ledger states plainly that the frame cannot be audited for rows outside itself, so no count is invented for that sense). The seven present school-related fields each have 11,600 nulls through the bound (`columnHealth.columns.*.frame.nullCount`). Those are not 11,600 absent rows, and the figure does not attribute a reason to a particular transaction lacking a field. On the association sample, the three distance regressors have zero nulls and zero recorded zeros; the score has a different distribution and is excluded (`columnHealth.columns`, `association.accessibilityExcluded`).

## One descriptive conditional association

The sole model in this edition is OLS of ln(PSF) on three shipped distance fields, with HC1 robust covariance (`association.estimator`, `association.distanceCoefficients`). The screen is the HDB 2021-01 through 2026-10 population within 2,000 metres of a primary-level school; the analysis uses complete cases and positive PSF (`association.screen`). The fit has 156,231 observations and 43 design columns (`association.sample`). Town fixed effects have 26 observed levels and ANG MO KIO as baseline; flat-type baseline is 4-ROOM, and year baseline is 2021 (`association.estimator.fixedEffects`). Continuous controls are floor area divided by 10, lease commencement centered on 2000 and divided by 10, and the mean storey value divided by 10 (`association.estimator.continuousControls`).

These are conditional associations holding the recorded controls fixed; they are not causal effects or admissions premiums. “Tier-1” in the second and third rows refers only to the corresponding manual list entries, not a verified admissions tier (`association.distanceCoefficients`). The two top-school distance regressors have correlation 0.7936; the plain primary-distance regressor correlates -0.1154 and -0.1828 with them (`association.regressorCorrelations`). The three coefficients are a correlated set in one specification, not independent effects.

The model excludes `school_accessibility_score`, because that score is screened as embedding the tier lists' policy-status columns. No model output here consumes SAP, gifted, IP, autonomous, or any tier-list policy field (`association.accessibilityExcluded`, `policyEligibility.associationExclusion`). The model's controls are physical transaction attributes and categorical town, flat-type and year effects; no policy eligibility variable is included (`association.estimator`, `policyEligibility.compliance`).

One specification was stated and all three distance coefficients are reported. No multiple-testing correction was applied, and no result was selected, dropped or promoted based on significance (`association.multipleTesting`). This reporting choice does not make the coefficients causal; statistical precision within this specification cannot repair absent treatment assignment, unobserved confounding, or the proxy nature of the tier lists.

## What this edition does not establish

- It does not establish a causal housing-price difference from proximity to a school or that any estimate is an admissions premium (`association.interpretation`). The association describes observed conditional relationships in one measured screen.
- It does not reproduce or validate the legacy's OLS, XGBoost, regional or RDD fits. The three legacy feature names are absent from the parquet schemas scanned for them, and no fitted model object or coefficient output for the legacy's fits was found on the surfaces this pack searched — the parquet schemas under `data/pipeline/` and `data/analysis/` and the stored model outputs under `data/analysis/feature_importance/`, whose matches are listed above (`legacyFeatureNames.schemaScan`, `adjacentModelArtefacts`); no scan of the checkout as a whole for fitted model objects exists, so nothing beyond those surfaces is claimed (`notMeasurable.legacyModelFit`, `notMeasurable.regionalFits`).
- It does not establish that the tier lists originated from an authoritative source, were produced at a particular time, or represent school quality independently of their recorded policy-status fields. They are screened as hand-authored reference data with origin unverified (`tierProvenance.origin`).
- It does not assign treatment to a transaction or draw conclusions from a treatment/control comparison. A coordinate-bearing file that is unlinked to the shipped rows adjudicates neither whether the shipped distances used it nor whether they did not (`schoolCoordinates.schoolsMoeGeojson.consumerScan.meaning`).
- It does not use the served plots as evidence about the legacy RDD fit in either direction. The RDD plot visibly shows a stepped-down fitted line across a printed 1,000 m rule over about 800–1,200 m (`rddRecovery.servedPlots.rddVisualization.visiblePrint`); the visible direction is recorded as what the pixels print, and it is not used to support, refute or rehabilitate the legacy's −$79.47 figure. The plot has no attached numeric table, no run identifier, and no linked record tying it to the legacy fit, so it is evidence neither for nor against that fit (`rddRecovery.servedPlots.rddVisualization.meaning`, `rddRecovery.servedPlots.unlinkedNote`).
- It does not interpret a recorded score zero as missingness, nor interpret missingness as a zero. The columns' null and zero counts remain separate (`columnHealth.states`, `columnHealth.columns`).
- It does not estimate an effect of policy eligibility, prescribe admissions decisions, or infer a buyer's eligibility from home location (`policyEligibility.compliance`).
- It does not establish that no artefact or analysis exists outside the bounded search roots. The scan boundaries and excluded surfaces are stated below and recorded in the ledger (`rddRecovery.tokenScan`, `multiplicityPolicy.legacyRecordedCorrection.scanBoundary`).
- It does not report a CV generalization gap, bandwidth sensitivity estimate, placebo estimate or balance test from a re-run. Those quantities are either unrecorded or unmeasurable in this checkout (`rddRecovery.legacyMethodStatements`, `rddRecovery.bandwidth`, `rddRecovery.covariateBalance`, `notMeasurable.spatialCv`).
- It does not turn a coefficient per 100 metres into a price amount for a dwelling. The output is on the natural log of PSF and is retained as a conditional association (`association.estimator`, `association.distanceCoefficients`).

## Reading this edition by persona

**Investors.** The measured coefficients do not support projecting a resale or rental return from school distance. The model outcome is ln(PSF), not rental yield, and the design is descriptive rather than causal (`association.estimator.response`, `association.interpretation`). The reported distance fields are not interchangeable with school quality: the ordinary primary-distance column has no tier or policy content, while the two “top school” distances use the lists whose origin is unverified (`association.distanceCoefficients`). A portfolio screen based on this model would overstate what is known.

**First-time buyers.** Treat location and school access as separate practical considerations, not as a measured premium in this edition. A distance in a shipped field describes the encoded distance proxy; it does not establish a place in an admissions boundary or an admissions outcome (`schoolCoordinates`, `rddRecovery.treatmentAssignment`). The accessibility score's many stored zeros are not nulls and the score is not used as an outcome or predictor in the reported association (`columnHealth.accessibilityZeroMass`, `association.accessibilityExcluded`).

**Upgraders.** The table is suitable for understanding the scope and limits of one descriptive model, not for comparing individual homes or estimating an increment to offer price. Its fixed effects and controls summarize the recorded sample; they do not remove every source of difference between neighborhoods or supply a credible boundary design (`association.estimator`, `association.interpretation`, `rddRecovery.treatmentAssignment`). Consider the tier labels as list-specific proxies only (`tierProvenance.origin`).

**Technical readers.** The measured screen, estimator, coefficient scale, field missingness and legacy-claim inventory are exposed in the ledger. The reference-data screen, the schema scan, and the text search each answer different questions. A schema's absent column is not a null observation; an unlinked artefact provides no lineage evidence in either direction; and a text search bounded by exclusions does not resolve material outside those surfaces (`columnHealth.states`, `schoolCoordinates.schoolsMoeGeojson.consumerScan.meaning`, `legacyFeatureNames.textScan`, `rddRecovery.tokenScan`). The producer's key resolution for claim references is syntactic: it checks that each key names a node, not whether the node substantively supports the claim (`claimKeyCheck`).
