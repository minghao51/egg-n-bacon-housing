## What the numbers are—and are not

This edition measures cross-sectional spatial association in planning-area appreciation, defined as `medianPsfW1 / medianPsfW0 - 1` over two 12-month windows that both stop at the measured cutoff (areaPanel.appreciationFormula; areaPanel.windowDefinition; areaPanel.windowMonths; cutoff.meaning). The global statistic is Moran's I on that variable (areaMoran.variable); the local statistics are local Moran (areaLisa.variable) and Getis-Ord Gi* (areaGiStar.variable), all built on the same recorded weights (areaMoran.weights.definition). A positive value of the global statistic would indicate that similar measured values tend to be associated under this particular spatial weights definition; the recorded value is negative and not significant (areaMoran.moranI; areaMoran.pSim). The local quadrants distinguish the relation of an area's standardized value to its spatial lag; the significance and multiplicity fields determine whether a quadrant receives a cluster membership (areaLisa.quadrantMapping; areaLisa.labelRule; areaLisa.multiplicityCorrection). The labels summarize the constructed statistic. They do not encode a causal mechanism, a price trajectory, a forecast, or buyer demand (notEstablished[N1]).

This is a two-window appreciation ratio, not a price level and not a return series over the frame's full history. A level of median sale PSF has no time difference built into it; the difference here is exactly the two recorded windows and nothing else (areaPanel.windowDefinition; areaPanel.medianDefinition). The legacy article's appreciation unit, population and interval are underspecified; its technical appendix names a rental input and H3 aggregation, whereas this remeasurement uses the unified transaction parquet and the planning-area two-window median-PSF ratio (legacyMethodRecovery.globalMoran.variable; legacyMethodRecovery.namedInput.scan; areaPanel.appreciationFormula). Accordingly neither the new values nor method choices validate the earlier appreciation statements (legacyMethodRecovery.globalMoran; legacyMethodRecovery.clusterTable; legacyMethodRecovery.spatialLagCorrelation).

The legacy served asset was inspected as a compressed JSON file of 1,156 bytes covering 41 areas (servedAssetInventory.path; servedAssetInventory.bytes; servedAssetInventory.areaCount). Its recorded first-area payload contains example fields for hotspot classification, a local-cluster object and neighbourhood effects, including transition/persistence-shaped values (servedAssetInventory.firstAreaByInsertionOrder.fields), and the per-area maps are constant across every area in the payload (servedAssetInventory.constantValueFields). That example is not a linked historical measurement record for the article, so it adjudicates neither for nor against the article's claims (servedAssetInventory.meaning; notEstablished[N3].statement). The provenance of that example is unresolved; origin unverified. No inference is made about the example or the former article from its structure.

Verbatim quotation is bounded to the frozen legacy article, which the ledger names and hashes (legacyQuoteCheck.legacyFile; legacyQuoteCheck.legacyFileSha256; legacyQuoteCheck.quotes). Absence evidence is a different thing and is recorded as four separate scans, each with its own roots, patterns, match lists and disclosed exclusions, set out in full under "Absence scans and their boundaries" below (absenceScans.dataSpatialArtefactScan; absenceScans.legacyNamedInput; absenceScans.methodImplementationScan; absenceScans.frameSchemaRentScan). ### Global statistic

Global Moran's I for the measured area-level appreciation is -0.070041 over 41 areas; its expected I under the recorded reference is -0.025 (areaMoran.moranI; areaMoran.n; areaMoran.expectedI; areaMoran.variable). The recorded permutation z is -0.703996 and the simulated p-value is 0.269, drawn from 999 permutations at seed 20261120 (areaMoran.zSim; areaMoran.pSim; areaMoran.permutations; areaMoran.seed). The analytic-normal and randomisation p-values are recorded as 0.464813 and 0.459827, with the standard error of I under normality 0.061620555 and a two-tailed test (areaMoran.pNorm; areaMoran.pRand; areaMoran.seI_norm; areaMoran.twoTailed). Under this protocol the appreciation screen is not significantly clustered across planning areas (areaMoran.meaning).

These values belong only to this method and this bounded population. They are not numerical corrections to the article's legacy global Moran or z claims: the ledger dispositions those claims not-reproducible, because the legacy's variable is unstated, its named input is absent, and the served payload cannot re-derive them (legacyClaims[C01].status; legacyClaims[C02].status; legacyMethodRecovery.globalMoran).

The reported permutation result should be read with the recorded finite permutation count and fixed seed, rather than as a probability that a particular area will rise or fall (areaMoran.permutations; areaMoran.seed; areaMoran.seedMechanism). It is a test summary conditional on the selected statistic, graph and tested data. No causal interpretation follows from statistical spatial association. The calculation does not separate common covariates, selection, housing composition, measurement error or unobserved spatial processes; these are outside the evidence produced here.

### Spatial weights and local inference

The graph uses eight nearest planning-area mean-coordinate neighbours, row-standardised; the recorded area graph has one connected component and zero island nodes, a mean of 8.0 neighbours per area and a total binary-graph weight of 328.0 (areaMoran.weights.kUsed; areaMoran.weights.definition; areaMoran.weights.transform; areaMoran.weights.connectedComponentsUndirected; areaMoran.weights.islands; areaMoran.weights.meanNeighbors; areaMoran.weights.s0). Nearest-neighbor weights create a computational neighborhood relation. They do not imply equal travel time, shared boundaries, identical housing markets or a physically contiguous urban fabric.

Local Moran statistics use the same recorded permutation count and seed as the global calculation (areaMoran.permutations; areaMoran.seed; areaLisa.implementation). The raw flag is evaluated at 0.05 and the adjusted labels at Benjamini–Hochberg q=0.05 over m=41 tests; the adjustment method and the Bonferroni threshold are recorded (areaLisa.significantUncorrectedAt005; areaLisa.multiplicityCorrection.q; areaLisa.multiplicityCorrection.m; areaLisa.multiplicityCorrection.method; areaLisa.multiplicityCorrection.bonferroniThreshold). A raw flag alone does not determine a membership: the membership records the result after adjustment, while retaining raw and adjusted values for review (areaLisa.labelRule). Before correction the quadrants count HH 9, HL 15, LH 10 and LL 7, and 3 areas are significant uncorrected at 0.05 (areaLisa.quadrantCountsUncorrected; areaLisa.significantUncorrectedAt005). A non-significant membership (`NS`) is not evidence of no spatial relationship in every formulation; it indicates that the local result did not satisfy this procedure's adjusted classification rule (areaLisa.labelRule).

There are 0 HH, 1 HL, 0 LH, 0 LL and 40 NS classifications in the analyzed areas (areaLisa.labelCounts). These are membership counts under this specific procedure, not counts from the legacy H3 analysis, nor outcome groups with observed future returns (legacyMethodRecovery.clusterTable.packReMeasurement). The complete per-area table that follows preserves the observed window counts and medians, quadrant, p-values and both significance flags. No additional aggregation over time is attempted.

A second local layer, Getis-Ord Gi* on the same variable and weights, records 1 hotspot, 0 coldspots and 40 not-significant areas over 41 areas (areaGiStar.labelCounts; areaGiStar.n; areaGiStar.variable). Its labels describe the measured window only and are not demand or return predictions (areaGiStar.meaning).

### How to read the uncertainty fields

The local pSim field is a simulated permutation result, not a direct measure of effect size. The adjusted value accounts for the family of local tests using the recorded Benjamini–Hochberg procedure (areaLisa.multiplicityCorrection.method; areaLisa.multiplicityCorrection.adjustedP). Both are presented so that readers can see what adjustment changes rather than receiving only a categorical label (areaLisa.members; areaLisa.multiplicityCorrection). The table's significance columns are boolean decisions under these fields; they do not quantify uncertainty in the underlying PSF median or guarantee stability under another sample, spatial partition, date bound, or model.

The permutation seed and count are part of the reproducibility record. Repeating the calculation requires the pinned inputs and the same implementation, dependency behavior and parameters; a matching seed alone is insufficient if any of those differ (areaMoran.seed; areaMoran.permutations; inputs; environment). The reported global permutation statistic and local simulated values should therefore be interpreted together with source hashes and cutoff metadata, not detached as portable constants.

### Source and method limitations

The transaction frame includes many columns beyond the selected analysis fields; recording the metadata schema prevents confusing an absent source field with a null value and makes clear that the analysis used named columns that exist (population.frameColumns; population.missingness.absentColumn). No claim is made that all source columns are complete or analytically relevant. The missingness counts above concern only the selected fields and the cutoff-bounded data, as specifically recorded by the population section (population.missingness).

A planning-area window median compresses variation among transactions in the same area. The local statistic then compares area-level values through graph weights. This two-stage design is not a hedonic model and does not control for dwelling type, age, tenure, floor, transaction mix or other property attributes. It cannot distinguish neighborhood-level price association from correlated compositional differences. The statistic is descriptive rather than an estimate of a causal neighborhood premium (areaPanel.medianDefinition; areaLisa.variable; areaMoran.variable).

The cutoff addresses the low-volume terminal month by applying the recorded comparison rule, but it does not repair other sources of temporal coverage bias or establish that all earlier dates have equal maturity. The terminal-month transaction count is a cutoff diagnostic and necessarily includes terminal month 2026-11; it is not an analysis aggregate (cutoff.terminalMonth; cutoff.terminalMonthTransactions). Analysis aggregates are bounded at the last complete month, 2026-10, and exclude later rows (cutoff.basis; cutoff.meaning; cutoff.lastCompleteMonth).

## Reading this edition by persona

### For an investor

Use the table as a compact description of where the two-window appreciation statistic had local association under this particular graph and adjustment. Do not translate an HH label into expected appreciation, resilience or demand. If the table is relevant to a screening question, keep the date boundary, composition limitations and local uncertainty fields beside the label. The local result is not a forecast, and no financial action follows from it alone (areaLisa.members; cutoff.lastCompleteMonth; areaLisa.multiplicityCorrection).

The global statistic summarizes spatial association at the chosen level; it does not establish a causal mechanism for the resemblance among area values and neighboring values. A practical reading is therefore conditional: the observed values have the recorded spatial pattern for the frozen source and method. Before considering a transaction, the buyer would need evidence specific to the property, period, comparison group and intended decision. This edition does not supply those separate analyses (areaMoran; areaPanel.medianDefinition).

### For a first-time buyer

Read each area's window medians as summaries of transactions rather than the price of a home you might purchase. A median can conceal the range and mix of homes within the area. A quadrant expresses a relationship in the constructed neighbor graph, not an assessment of whether an individual home is fairly priced or suitable. The table preserves the underlying window transaction counts so that the scale of each area summary is visible (areaPanel.members).

An NS membership should not be read as “no neighborhood effect”; it means the area did not receive a significant local membership after the recorded correction. Conversely, a significant membership is not proof of future price behavior. Keep both readings bounded to the measured period and do not substitute them for property-specific due diligence (areaLisa.multiplicityCorrection; areaLisa.members; cutoff.meaning).

### For an upgrader

A move often depends on timing and a resale horizon. This edition has no repeated, comparable area statistic from which to assess persistence or transition. The legacy persistence phrases are withdrawn because the inspected article/asset boundary does not provide a reproducible time-series measure; this is not a claim that relevant information cannot exist outside that boundary (legacyMethodRecovery.transition; absenceScans.dataSpatialArtefactScan; notEstablished[N5]). The present snapshot cannot answer whether an area's relative position will remain the same over a planned holding period.

For a choice involving current and future homes, this table can serve only as a context note about a bounded two-window appreciation pattern. It does not compare the expected resale outcomes of the two homes, estimate timing risk, or identify catalysts. This edition makes no claim about catalysts; evaluating such a question would require distinct data and methods and is outside its measurements (areaLisa.variable; areaLisa.members).
